# NotebookLM

Your AI-powered research and knowledge assistant by Google

transition: slide-up

# What is NotebookLM?

**NotebookLM** is an AI research assistant that works exclusively with **your sources**.

<v-clicks>

- Built by Google, powered by Gemini
- Upload documents — NotebookLM becomes an expert on them
- Answers are **grounded in your content**, not the open web
- Ideal for research, studying, and knowledge management

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

> It doesn't hallucinate facts from the internet — it only uses what you give it.

</v-click>

---
transition: slide-up
---

# What Can You Upload?

<v-clicks>

- **PDFs** — papers, reports, books
- **Google Docs & Slides** — your own documents
- **Web URLs** — articles and pages
- **YouTube URLs** — it reads transcripts
- **Audio files** — meetings, podcasts
- **Text / Markdown** — notes and drafts

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

Up to **50 sources** per notebook, up to **500,000 words** each.

</v-click>

---
transition: slide-up
---

# Core Features

<v-clicks>

- **Chat with your sources** — ask anything, get cited answers
- **Notebook Guide** — auto-generated FAQ, briefing doc, and study guide
- **Inline citations** — every answer links back to the exact source passage
- **Source highlights** — click a citation to jump to the original text
- **Cross-source synthesis** — ask questions that span multiple documents

</v-clicks>

transition: slide-up

# Audio Overview

One of NotebookLM's standout features: **generate a podcast from your sources**.

<v-clicks>

- Two AI hosts discuss your documents in a natural, conversational tone
- Great for absorbing dense material on the go
- Customisable — tell it to focus on specific topics or target a specific audience
- Output is a downloadable MP3

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

> Turn a 50-page report into a 10-minute podcast in one click.

</v-click>

transition: slide-up

# The Notebook Guide

When you add sources, NotebookLM auto-generates a **Notebook Guide**:

| Section | What it contains |
|---------|-----------------|
| **FAQ** | Common questions answered from your sources |
| **Study Guide** | Key concepts + quiz questions |
| **Table of Contents** | Structured outline of the material |
| **Briefing Doc** | Executive summary of all sources |
| **Timeline** | Chronological events (if applicable) |

# Saving Notes & Highlights

NotebookLM is also a **note-taking workspace**:

<v-clicks>

- Save any AI response as a **note** with one click
- Write your own notes alongside AI-generated ones
- Pin key passages from sources
- Notes can be used as sources for further questions
- Export notes as a Google Doc

</v-clicks>

# Best Use Cases

<v-clicks>

- **Research** — synthesise multiple papers into a clear summary
- **Studying** — generate quiz questions from lecture notes
- **Legal / compliance** — query contracts and policies without reading every line
- **Onboarding** — upload company docs, ask questions as a new employee
- **Content creation** — use sources to draft grounded articles and reports

</v-clicks>
