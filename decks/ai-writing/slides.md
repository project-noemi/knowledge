- Writing takes **too long** for most professionals

- Editing and rewriting cycles eat up hours

# The Core Technique — Give Context

Bad prompt:
```
Write an email about the project update.
```

Good prompt:
```
Write a concise project status email to a non-technical stakeholder.
The project is 2 weeks behind schedule due to a supplier delay.
We're back on track and expect delivery by March 15.
Tone: professional but reassuring. Max 150 words.
```

<v-click>

**The more context you give, the less editing you do.**

</v-click>

# Rewriting & Editing

AI is excellent at improving existing text:

<v-clicks>

- **Shorten it** — "Reduce this to 3 sentences without losing the key point"
- **Change tone** — "Rewrite this to sound more confident and direct"
- **Simplify** — "Explain this to someone with no technical background"
- **Formalise** — "Make this more appropriate for a board presentation"
- **Proofread** — "Fix grammar and improve clarity, keep my wording where possible"

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

> Paste your draft and ask for exactly what you need. You stay in control.

</v-click>

# Email Workflows

<v-clicks>

**Draft from bullet points:**
> "Turn these notes into a professional email: [paste notes]"

**Reply to a difficult message:**
> "Draft a polite but firm response to this email: [paste email]"

**Follow-up:**
> "Write a short follow-up email — we met last Tuesday, I'm following up on the proposal I sent"

**Cold outreach:**
> "Write a concise cold email to a potential client in retail who might benefit from our inventory management software"

</v-clicks>

# Summarising Long Content

<v-clicks>

- Paste a long report → "Summarise this in 5 bullet points for an executive"
- Paste meeting transcript → "Extract action items and owners from this transcript"
- Paste a research paper → "Explain the key findings in plain language"
- Paste a contract → "What are the key obligations and risks in this document?"

</v-clicks>

# Maintaining Your Voice

<v-clicks>

- Give AI examples of your writing: "Here are 3 emails I've written — match this tone and style"
- Tell it what to avoid: "Don't use corporate buzzwords or filler phrases"
- Set constraints: "Write as if you're a confident but approachable founder"
- Always review and edit — AI drafts are **starting points**, not final copies

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

**Save your best prompt as a template** — reuse it every time you need that type of content.

</v-click>

# Content Calendar in Minutes

Example workflow for social media:

<v-clicks>

1. Describe your business and audience in one paragraph
2. Ask for 10 LinkedIn post ideas for this month
3. Pick 3, ask for full drafts
4. Review, edit to match your voice
5. Schedule with Buffer or Hootsuite

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

What used to take a full day now takes **under an hour**.

</v-click>
