# The Marketing Challenge

<v-clicks>

- Content demand has never been higher — every channel needs fresh material
- Personalisation at scale was impossible without large teams
- Campaign testing is slow when creative production is expensive
- Sales teams spend more time on admin than actual selling

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

AI doesn't just speed up existing work — it makes entirely new approaches possible.

</v-click>

# Content Creation at Scale

<v-clicks>

- Write 10 versions of an ad headline in 30 seconds
- Generate a month of social media posts in one session
- Create blog posts, email newsletters, and case study drafts
- Repurpose one piece of content into multiple formats
- Translate campaigns into multiple languages automatically

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

**Example:**
> "Take this blog post and turn it into: a LinkedIn post, a Twitter thread, an email newsletter intro, and a 30-second video script"

</v-click>

# SEO & Organic Growth

<v-clicks>

- **Keyword research** — "What are the top search questions for [topic]?"
- **Content briefs** — AI outlines articles optimised for search intent
- **Meta descriptions** — generate for every page on your site
- **Internal linking suggestions** — identify gaps in your content architecture
- **Competitor analysis** — summarise what competitors rank for

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

**Tools:** Semrush AI, Surfer SEO, ChatGPT + manual research, Ahrefs AI features

</v-click>

# Email Marketing

<v-clicks>

- **Subject lines** — generate 10 options, A/B test the best
- **Personalisation at scale** — "Write a version of this email for each industry segment: retail, healthcare, finance"
- **Drip sequences** — outline and draft entire email journeys
- **Re-engagement campaigns** — "Write an email to win back customers who haven't bought in 6 months"
- **Analyse performance** — ask AI to interpret your campaign metrics

</v-clicks>

transition: slide-up

# Ad Copy & Campaign Creative

<v-clicks>

- Generate 20 ad variations in minutes for A/B testing
- Match copy to the awareness stage: cold audience vs warm retargeting
- Write platform-specific copy: Google Ads (short), LinkedIn (professional), Meta (conversational)
- Create landing page copy aligned to ad messaging
- Generate product descriptions at scale

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

**Example:**
> "Write 5 Facebook ad variations for our project management tool. Target: small business owners. Hook: wasted time in meetings. CTA: Start free trial."

</v-click>

# Sales Enablement

<v-clicks>

- **Prospect research** — summarise a company before a sales call
- **Cold outreach** — personalised emails at scale
- **Objection handling** — "What are the top 5 objections to our pricing and how should I respond?"
- **Proposal drafts** — first draft from a discovery call summary
- **Follow-up emails** — contextual, timely, and human-sounding

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

**Example:**
> "I have a call with the Head of Operations at a 200-person logistics company. Summarise the key challenges logistics companies face right now and suggest 3 questions to open the conversation."

</v-click>

# CRM & Lead Management

<v-clicks>

- **Lead scoring** — AI identifies which leads are most likely to convert
- **CRM data enrichment** — fill in missing company and contact details automatically
- **Meeting summaries to CRM** — auto-log call notes from your meeting AI
- **Pipeline analysis** — "Which deals in my pipeline are at risk of slipping?"
- **Follow-up reminders** — AI suggests the next best action for each deal

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

**Tools:** HubSpot AI, Salesforce Einstein, Clay, Apollo.io

</v-click>

# Customer Personas & Market Research

<v-clicks>

- Generate detailed customer personas from your existing customer data
- Analyse customer reviews for recurring themes and pain points
- Map the customer journey and identify friction points
- Summarise competitor positioning from their website and reviews
- Generate survey questions and analyse open-text responses

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

**Example:**
> "Here are 50 customer reviews. Group the feedback into themes and tell me the top 3 pain points and top 3 things customers love."

</v-click>
