How to identify opportunities, lead adoption, and build an AI-ready team

# Why Strategy Matters

<v-clicks>

- Most organisations are experimenting with AI — few are transforming with it
- Without a strategy, AI adoption is scattered, inconsistent, and hard to measure
- The biggest risk isn't using AI wrong — it's **your competitors using it while you don't**
- A clear strategy aligns tools, people, and business outcomes

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

AI strategy is not an IT decision. It's a **business decision**.

</v-click>

# The AI Opportunity Framework

Before choosing a tool, ask three questions:

<v-clicks>

1. **Where do we spend the most time on low-value tasks?**
   → These are your highest-ROI automation targets

2. **Where do we make decisions slowly due to lack of information?**
   → AI can accelerate research, analysis, and synthesis

3. **Where does quality suffer because of scale or speed constraints?**
   → AI can maintain quality at higher volume

</v-clicks>

# The AI Adoption Stages

<v-clicks>

**Stage 1 — Individual use**
Each person uses AI for their own tasks independently

**Stage 2 — Team workflows**
Shared prompts, templates, and AI-assisted processes across the team

**Stage 3 — Integrated systems**
AI built into your tools, CRM, and data pipelines — automated and measurable

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

Most organisations are at Stage 1. Moving to Stage 2 is where ROI multiplies.

</v-click>

# Calculating ROI

AI ROI comes from three sources:

<v-clicks>

- **Time savings** — hours saved × hourly cost × number of people × frequency
- **Quality improvement** — fewer errors, better outputs, higher conversion
- **New capabilities** — things you couldn't do before (personalisation at scale, 24/7 support)

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

**Example:**
> 10 sales reps each save 1 hour/day on research and admin = 50 hours/week
> At €50/hr average = €2,500/week = **€130,000/year** saved from one use case

</v-click>

# Building Your AI Toolkit Strategy

<v-clicks>

1. **Audit** — list every tool your team currently uses
2. **Check** — which already have AI features you're not using?
3. **Prioritise** — pick 1–2 use cases with the highest time savings
4. **Pilot** — run a 30-day test with a small team
5. **Measure** — track time saved and quality change
6. **Scale** — roll out what works, drop what doesn't

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

**Start with what you already pay for.** Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini, and HubSpot AI may already be in your licences.

</v-click>

# Change Management

The tools are easy. The people are hard.

<v-clicks>

- Some employees fear AI will replace their jobs — address this directly
- Frame AI as **removing the boring parts**, not the job itself
- Start with willing early adopters — let results speak
- Create internal champions who can help others
- Share wins visibly — celebrate time saved, not just cost cut

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

The fastest path to adoption is **one colleague showing another something useful**.

</v-click>

# Building AI Literacy in Your Team

<v-clicks>

- **Awareness** — everyone understands what AI can and can't do
- **Proficiency** — each person can use AI tools relevant to their role
- **Creation** — some people build workflows and custom tools
- **Leadership** — someone owns AI strategy and keeps the organisation learning

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

You don't need everyone to be an AI expert. You need everyone to be **AI-comfortable**.

</v-click>

# Governance — What You Need

<v-clicks>

- **Acceptable use policy** — what tools are approved, what data can be shared
- **Data privacy guidelines** — especially for GDPR and customer data
- **Review process** — who signs off on AI-generated content going external
- **Vendor assessment** — how you evaluate new AI tools before adoption
- **Training requirement** — minimum AI literacy for all employees

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

A one-page AI policy beats a 50-page one that nobody reads.

</v-click>

# The 90-Day AI Strategy Plan

<v-clicks>

**Month 1 — Discover**
Audit current tool usage. Survey team on pain points. Identify top 3 use cases.

**Month 2 — Pilot**
Run focused pilots on 1–2 use cases. Measure time savings. Collect feedback.

**Month 3 — Scale**
Roll out what worked. Create internal guides. Appoint an AI champion. Plan next quarter.

</v-clicks>

# Summary

<v-clicks>

Start with **where time is wasted** — those are your highest-ROI AI opportunities

Move from individual use → **team workflows** → integrated systems

**Measure ROI** from day one — time saved, quality improved, new capabilities

Address people and culture — **change management matters as much as tools**

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

*AI doesn't transform businesses. People with a strategy do.*

</v-click>
