# Meeting Intelligence

AI meeting tools join your call, transcribe everything, and produce structured summaries automatically.

<v-clicks>

- **Otter.ai** — real-time transcription, highlights, action items
- **Fireflies.ai** — records, transcribes, and lets you search past meetings
- **Fathom** — free meeting recorder with instant summaries
- **Tactiq** — Chrome extension for Google Meet and Zoom
- **Microsoft Copilot** — built into Teams for Microsoft 365 users

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

**Setup:** Connect to your Google Calendar. The bot joins automatically. You focus on the conversation.

</v-click>

# Email & Inbox Management

<v-clicks>

- **ChatGPT / Claude** — draft replies, summarise long threads, write cold outreach
- **Microsoft Copilot in Outlook** — summarise threads, draft replies inline
- **Gemini in Gmail** — "Help me write" button, summarise long emails
- **Superhuman** — AI triage, instant replies, keyboard-first inbox
- **SaneBox** — AI filters unimportant emails automatically

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

**Quick win:** Paste a long email thread into Claude and ask: "Summarise this thread and tell me what I need to action."

</v-click>

# Building Your AI Productivity Stack

<v-clicks>

1. **Meeting notes** → Otter.ai or Fireflies (free tier available)
2. **Email drafts** → Gemini in Gmail or Copilot in Outlook
3. **Calendar** → Reclaim.ai for focus time protection
4. **Notes & docs** → Notion AI or ChatGPT for summarisation
5. **General tasks** → Claude or ChatGPT for anything else

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

Start with **one tool** and build the habit before adding the next.
