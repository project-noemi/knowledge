# How It Works

<v-clicks>

- You write a **text prompt** describing what you want
- The AI model generates an image or video from your description
- You refine with follow-up prompts or settings
- Download and use

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

```
"A minimalist office scene with a laptop and coffee cup,
morning light, blue and white colour palette, photorealistic"
```

→ Professional-quality image in seconds.

# Writing Good Image Prompts

A strong image prompt includes:

<v-clicks>

- **Subject** — what is in the image
- **Style** — photorealistic, illustration, watercolour, flat design
- **Lighting** — natural light, studio, golden hour, dramatic
- **Mood** — professional, warm, minimalist, bold
- **Colour palette** — blue tones, monochrome, brand colours
- **Composition** — close-up, wide shot, bird's eye view

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

```
"Professional woman presenting in a modern boardroom,
confident expression, blue brand colours, clean background,
corporate photography style, shallow depth of field"
```

</v-click>

# Video Generation Tools

<v-clicks>

- **Runway Gen-3** — text or image to video, professional quality
- **Sora** — OpenAI's video model, highly realistic scenes
- **Kling AI** — high-quality video from image or text
- **Pika** — quick video clips from prompts or images

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

AI video is ideal for **explainers, product demos, social content, and training videos** — without a film crew.

# Copyright and Commercial Use

<v-clicks>

- **Adobe Firefly** — trained on licensed Adobe Stock, safe for commercial use
- **Canva AI** — commercially licensed output included with paid plans
- **Midjourney / DALL-E / Sora** — check terms of service for commercial rights
- **Always check** the platform's ToS before using AI images commercially
- Watermark removal ≠ commercial licence

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

When in doubt, use Adobe Firefly or Canva for anything going into paid advertising.

</v-click>
